WHITE ROSES: background pictures
================================
Put your own background pictures in this folder with exactly these names. Every one is optional:
a screen without a picture is plain white.

  bg-cover.webp     the envelope screen (screen 1)
  bg-photo.webp     photo, date and "save the date" (screen 2)
  bg-venue.webp     venue (screen 3)
  bg-timeline.webp  timeline (screen 4)
  bg-details.webp   details (screen 5)
  bg-rsvp.webp      RSVP (screen 6)
  bg-closing.webp   closing (screen 7)

Size: portrait, ratio 9 : 15 (for example 1020 x 1700 px). Keep each file under about 300 KB (WebP, quality ~80).
Leave the middle of the picture calm, because the text sits there. The envelope itself is drawn by the site (plain paper).
